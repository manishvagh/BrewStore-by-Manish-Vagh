# BrewStore trademark guidelines

**BrewStore** and **Manish Vagh** (as used to identify the BrewStore product) are trademarks and brand identifiers of Manish Vagh.

These guidelines apply in addition to the [MIT License](./LICENSE). The MIT License covers **copyright in the software**. It does **not** grant permission to use the BrewStore name, logos, or “by Manish Vagh” branding in a way that confuses users about the official product.

Inspired by common open-source brand practice (including GitLab’s trademark model): **code may be forked; the brand may not be impersonated.**

## Official product

The official BrewStore project is only:

- Site: [brewstore.app](https://brewstore.app/)
- Source: [github.com/manishvagh/BrewStore-by-Manish-Vagh](https://github.com/manishvagh/BrewStore-by-Manish-Vagh)
- Author: [manishvagh.in](https://manishvagh.in/)
- Releases published by **@manishvagh** from that repository

Anything else is a fork, remix, or third-party build — not the official BrewStore.

## Allowed

- Using, modifying, and redistributing the software under the MIT License (keep the copyright and permission notices).
- Truthful statements such as:  
  `This product is based on BrewStore by Manish Vagh.`
- Linking to the official repository or website.
- Contributing upstream via pull requests to the official repo.

## Not allowed (without prior written permission)

- Using **BrewStore**, **BrewStore by Manish Vagh**, or confusingly similar names as the name of a fork, product, company, app listing, domain, or social account in a way that suggests it *is* the official BrewStore.
- Using BrewStore logos, icons, or wordmarks as your own product branding.
- Claiming endorsement, partnership, or authorship by Manish Vagh when that is not true.
- Removing or replacing author attribution in a way that presents a modified build as the original official app.

## If you distribute a modified version

1. Choose a **different product name**.
2. Keep the MIT copyright notice for Manish Vagh’s original work.
3. Include a clear statement, for example:

   > This product is based on BrewStore by Manish Vagh.  
   > BREWSTORE is a trademark of Manish Vagh.  
   > This build is not the official BrewStore and is not affiliated with Manish Vagh.

4. Do not reuse the official icon/logo as your primary brand mark unless you have permission.

## In the official app and repo

Attribution to Manish Vagh (sidebar, Credits, documentation, package metadata) is part of the official product identity. Pull requests that remove or weaken that attribution without an approved reason will be rejected.

## Questions

For trademark or branding permission requests, open an issue on the official repository or contact the author via [manishvagh.in](https://manishvagh.in/).
